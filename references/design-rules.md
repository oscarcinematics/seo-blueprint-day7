# Design rules

The look comes from the reference, not from this file. In order of authority:

1. The site's "## Design" section in its CLAUDE.md (the brief written from the references Oscar gave).
2. The kit the site was copied from.

Copy what they do: type, weights, sizes, colour, spacing, layout and motion. Never "correct" a reference to fit a general rule.

**Photos**
- Client photos first. Stock only with the owner's OK.
- The weakest photo sets the impression. Cut a weak shot rather than fill a slot.
- Each photo once per page.

**Before showing anything**
- Run `node code/design/shoot.mjs --all`, reload one page mid-scroll, and look at every shot.
- Nothing scrolls sideways at 390.
- With reduced motion or JavaScript off, the page still shows everything.
- Fix a complaint by finding its cause, never by doing the opposite. Mock two options and show the better one.
