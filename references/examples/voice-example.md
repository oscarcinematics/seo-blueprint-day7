# PART 2 - Your material

The words only this business can use. Filled in from your writing, your calls and your reviews.

**Reference page** - the approved page every other page should sound like.
- `/emergency-plumbing-toronto`

**Your dials** - change any by saying so.
- **Humour:** light on blogs, one line on GBP posts, almost none on service pages
- **Person:** "we" for the business, "you" for the reader
- **Sentence length:** short
- **Formality:** casual, professional

**Words you use** - your own words for your work and services.
- "the crew", "the techs", never "our team of experts"
- "upfront, in writing" for pricing
- "a normal daytime visit" for anything that can wait
- Canadian spelling: odour, labour, neighbour, litre

**Words you never use** - words you or your customers dislike.
- "cheap" (customers hear "cut corners")
- "minor issue" about a leak (to the homeowner it never is)
- delve, leverage, game-changer, robust

**How you describe what you do, in your own words** - how you'd explain it to a customer.
- "We fix it, we tell you the price before we start, and the price doesn't change."
- "Most jobs are done in one visit, because the truck carries the parts."

**Your real phrases** - things you've actually said, not invented.
- "Put a bucket under it and book a normal daytime visit. It'll still be there in the morning."
- "Not on our watch."
- "We've seen worse, and we've pulled worse out of a drain."

Stories and numbers live in `context/proof/proof-inventory.md`. Who you sell to lives in `context/buyers.md`.
